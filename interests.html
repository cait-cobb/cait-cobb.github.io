Interests

What I care about beyond the work itself.

Health wearables and physiological data

Wearable sensors can give people real insight into their own health, and there are a growing number of stories about devices flagging conditions their users didn't know they had. As a biomedical engineer and lifelong athlete, I'm interested in the data itself: what it can reliably tell us, and how to turn it into information people can act on.

Sports: Performance and Teamwork

  • NCAA Division I rowing, Bucknell University

I'm a life long competitive athlete with a love for teamwork, collaboration, and constantly improving. I rowed for Bucknell's Division I women's team for four years and competed at all Patriot League Championships during my college career. This was after captaining the cross country and swimming teams in high school, where I received the Henry Prize for service to athletic teams. Years of team training are a big part of why I care about health and performance data.

Teaching and coaching

I've coached competitive swimmers from ages 5 to 18, taught lessons at the YMCA, and co-led an adapted aquatics program where 40 instructors taught 90 children with disabilities how to swim. Sports have been a central part of my life, and I enjoy helping others develop their skills and confidence through athletic participation.

Mentoring and community

At Johnson & Johnson I've mentored interns and co-ops through the JumpStart program and helped publish the TLDP Council newsletter, interviewing colleagues and sharing their stories.
