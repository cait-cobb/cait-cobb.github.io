Finite element model of cerebrospinal fluid flow

Bucknell University, Spring 2020

Modeled cerebrospinal fluid flow through the brain and cervical spine in COMSOL, comparing healthy anatomy with cysts of different sizes and with the thicker fluid seen in meningitis.

Presented as a research poster.
